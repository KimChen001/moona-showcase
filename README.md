## A ritual you take part in

You shuffle. You pick. The reading unfolds when you are ready. The interface makes room for the moment before an answer, with an animated orb, card interactions, and options to reduce or switch off decorative motion.

## A sky that changes with the day

The astronomy is calculated; the interpretation is a separate layer. MOONA distinguishes calculated facts, live AI, saved AI replies, and offline library content so you can tell what you are looking at.

![The current sky on MOONA's zodiac wheel](assets/todays-sky.png)

## Try it in a minute

1. Open **[moona.fun](https://moona.fun/)** on a phone or desktop. No app download is needed.
2. Browse the Book, explore the sky, or start with a card.
3. A limited guest AI trial is available. When prompted, sign in with an email verification code to continue within the demo's usage limits. No password is needed.

The demo is evolving. AI replies can be mistaken, and availability is subject to usage limits. Tarot and astrology are offered for **reflection and entertainment**, not as medical, legal, or financial advice.

## Your space, with clear boundaries

- Readings, full conversation history, notes, and birth details are saved in the browser where you use MOONA. Email sign-in does **not** sync these records between devices.
- When you use AI, the context needed for that request passes through our server to the AI provider. Accepted replies are cached for a limited period; they may include your words.
- Signing in stores an email account and session on the server. Usage identifiers can be linked to that account; this is not an anonymous messaging service.
- Receiving product updates is a separate opt-in, not a condition of signing in.

Read the current [data explanation](https://moona.fun/about#sent-title) before sharing personal information. For a summary of the current design and its limits, see [Data and privacy](docs/data-and-privacy.md).

## Under the surface

MOONA combines a responsive Next.js interface, astronomical calculations, a curated bilingual content library, and server-side AI generation with structured output validation. A PostgreSQL ledger controls AI usage and spending; API keys stay on the server. Validation reduces some errors and does not guarantee that every interpretation is correct.

This repository is the public product showcase. It contains presentation material and product screenshots; the application source and operational data are maintained separately.
